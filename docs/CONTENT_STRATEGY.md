# Saeid KING Content Strategy

## Objective

Position Saeid KING as a serious Arabic-language media channel with a clean, sponsor-ready web presence.

## Audience

The site should serve viewers, returning subscribers, sponsors, and possible collaborators.

## Brand promise

Saeid KING explains complex events with calm structure, visual storytelling, and careful wording.

## Editorial pillars

1. Global analysis.
2. Economy and technology.
3. Visual storytelling.
4. Clear summaries for YouTube and mobile viewers.

## Guardrails

1. Separate facts from analysis and assumptions.
2. Avoid fake urgency and inflated claims.
3. Use real YouTube links only after verification.
4. Use audience numbers only when copied from YouTube Studio.
5. Keep legal, personal, and administrative matters outside the public media brand.
