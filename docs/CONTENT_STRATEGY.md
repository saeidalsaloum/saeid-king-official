# Saeid KING content strategy

## Positioning

Saeid KING is a serious Arabic personal media brand focused on structured analysis, visual storytelling, calm explanation, and professional public communication. The channel should feel premium through restraint: clear framing, strong pacing, careful claims, and a visual identity that supports the story.

## Core promise

Help Arabic-speaking viewers understand public topics with more context and less noise. Every script, thumbnail, title, and description should answer a simple question: what will the viewer understand better after watching?

## Voice

- Calm, proud, direct, and professional.
- Clear before clever.
- Strong through structure, specificity, and restraint, not hype.
- Serious without sounding cold; confident without pretending to know everything.
- No exaggerated claims, invented authority, fake social proof, or unverifiable performance language.

## Content pillars

- Public stories and events explained in accessible Arabic.
- Political, social, cultural, and human-interest context presented carefully.
- Visual storytelling that supports understanding through maps, timelines, clean typography, restrained pacing, and purposeful contrast.
- Structured analysis that separates facts, context, interpretation, and open questions.
- Sponsor-safe communication that protects audience trust and makes disclosures clear.

## Script and packaging principles

- Start with the viewer question, not with noise.
- Build each video around a clear narrative path: setup, context, tension, explanation, and takeaway.
- Use thumbnails and titles to create curiosity without false promises or panic language.
- Keep visual elements cinematic, calm, and readable on mobile.
- Prefer precise claims that can be supported over broad claims that sound impressive.
- Avoid fake urgency unless the upload is genuinely time-sensitive and verified.

## Editorial boundaries

The channel should not publish private personal data, family material, legal case files, personal administration documents, or advice that sounds legal, financial, medical, banking, insurance, migration, or authority-related.

Commercial collaboration must be transparent, relevant to the audience, and compatible with the channel’s calm public tone. Audience numbers, upload schedules, sponsorship claims, partnerships, achievements, and channel statistics must be verified before public use.
