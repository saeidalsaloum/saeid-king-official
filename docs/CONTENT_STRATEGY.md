# Content Strategy

## Positioning

Saeid KING should read as a serious Arabic media channel with a calm, credible, and sponsor-ready public presence. The website is a concise official reference point, not a sales funnel or a personal archive.

## Voice

- Arabic-first, direct, and restrained.
- Professional without inflated claims.
- Clear about collaboration boundaries and contact method.
- Respectful of audience trust, privacy, and editorial fit.

## Collaboration copy

The collaboration page should help serious partners understand how to make a relevant request. It may describe preferred request details, editorial fit, and communication boundaries. It must not invent audience size, sponsor history, testimonials, press coverage, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Safety boundaries

Do not publish private personal data, legal case material, family matters, banking, insurance, medical, migration, authority, or personal administration material. Public contact should remain email-only through `saeidkingofficial@gmail.com`.

## Maintenance checklist

- Keep every HTML page RTL with `lang="ar"` and `dir="rtl"`.
- Keep all assets local and lightweight.
- Keep CSS readable and maintainable rather than minified.
- Avoid trackers, forms, cookies, third-party embeds, external scripts, remote fonts, and new dependencies.
- Remove temporary or experimental files before release.
