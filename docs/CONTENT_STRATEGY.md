# Saeid KING content strategy

## Positioning

Saeid KING is a serious Arabic personal media brand focused on structured analysis, visual storytelling, calm explanation, and professional public communication.

## Voice

- Calm, proud, direct, and professional.
- Clear before clever.
- Strong through restraint and specificity, not hype.
- No exaggerated claims, invented authority, or fake social proof.

## Content focus

- Public stories and events explained in accessible Arabic.
- Political, social, cultural, and human-interest context presented carefully.
- Visual storytelling that supports understanding.
- Sponsor-safe communication that protects audience trust.

## Editorial boundaries

The channel should not publish private personal data, family material, legal case files, personal administration documents, or advice that sounds legal, financial, medical, banking, insurance, migration, or authority-related.

Commercial collaboration must be transparent, relevant to the audience, and compatible with the channel’s calm public tone.
