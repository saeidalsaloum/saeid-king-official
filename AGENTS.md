# Repository Instructions

This repository hosts the official static Saeid KING website for GitHub Pages.

## Release-safety rules

- Keep the site static: plain HTML, CSS, SVG, and documentation only unless explicitly approved.
- Do not add forms, cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, third-party embeds, external scripts, remote fonts, or new runtime dependencies.
- Use email-only public contact through `saeidkingofficial@gmail.com`.
- Do not use legacy or unofficial contact email addresses.
- Do not add phone contact details, private personal data, family material, banking, insurance, medical, migration, authority, or personal administration content.
- Do not add public dispute material, legal case material, or private documentation.
- Do not invent or publish fake sponsor logos, testimonials, press mentions, video links, audience metrics, statistics, or unverifiable claims.
- Do not create or restore temporary, scratch, probe, test, placeholder, or kit files.
- Do not reintroduce removed pages such as `kit.html` or other experimental HTML files.
- Preserve GitHub Pages compatibility from the repository root.

## Content tone

- Keep public copy calm, credible, sponsor-ready, and restrained.
- Avoid hype, artificial urgency, exaggerated superiority, and manipulative collaboration language.
- Prefer concise Arabic copy with clear RTL support and accessible structure.

## Verification expectations

Before finalizing changes, confirm local links and assets resolve, HTML pages use `lang="ar"` and `dir="rtl"`, and the site has no forbidden contact, tracking, temporary, private, or unverifiable material.
