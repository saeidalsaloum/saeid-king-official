This repository is the public website for the Saeid KING YouTube channel.

Rules for future Codex work:

1. Keep the site static and GitHub Pages friendly.
2. Use Arabic-first RTL layout.
3. Do not add public contact numbers, public dispute material, family details, banking details, medical details, residency material, or authority files.
4. Do not add fake video links, fake sponsor logos, fake testimonials, or unverified audience numbers.
5. Do not add external scripts, trackers, forms, cookies, analytics, or remote fonts without a separate review.
6. Keep copy serious, calm, credible, and sponsor-ready.
7. Prefer small pull requests with clear summaries and manual test notes.
8. Do not mark any PR ready for review, merge, or deploy without Saeid’s manual approval.
